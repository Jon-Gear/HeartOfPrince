

Story Outline – Prince’s First Arc

Character

Prince has suffered a heart attack and has approximately two years left to live. He knows that if he dies, the Alley Cats will be lost forever to the streets.

Goal

Secure the future of the Alley Cats before he dies.

Problem

Prince himself does not know what he is supposed to do. He has no answers, no plan, and no experience outside of crime to guide him.

Choice / Action

Prince recalls Munir, a man he briefly met in the hospital whose way of life left a strong impression on him. 

Believing Munir might understand how to make good decisions in life, Prince seeks him out at the mosque and asks him directly how he consistently knows the right thing to do.

Get Goal

Munir explains that the guidance he follows comes from Islam. He tells Prince that the religion provides principles and advice for living a good life.

Prince recognizes that much of what Munir describes seems to be wise and beneficial.

Pay for Getting Goal, Major Conflict

Prince’s underlying atheistic assumptions cause him to resist the idea. 

He accepts that the advice seems good in practice, but questions how it can be trusted as objectively true or universally valid.

Pivotal Choice

Rather than rejecting the claim immediately, Prince honestly follows the chain of reasoning behind it. 

Munir claims that Islam comes from a Creator, and that this Creator is the source of moral guidance.

Prince decides to seriously examine whether such a Creator exists. He examines the evidence and reflects on the signs of creation.

Through sincere contemplation, he becomes convinced that there is a Creator.

Changed Story World

Prince’s circumstances remain the same—he is still dying, and the Alley Cats are still vulnerable.

However, his worldview has fundamentally changed.

“There is a Creator. And He can help me.”
