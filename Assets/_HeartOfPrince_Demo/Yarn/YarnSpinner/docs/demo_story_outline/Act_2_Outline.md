Step 1: Character

“Maybe there’s another way to live.”

Prince seeks out Munir, believing he has found a man whose life reflects the kind of wisdom and purpose Prince himself lacks.

Step 2: Goal

“I want to know how you make your decisions.”

Prince wants to understand the principles that guide Munir’s life. He hopes those same principles might help him make better decisions for himself and for the Alley Cats.

Step 3: Problem

“What’s your secret?”

Prince realizes that unless he understands the source of Munir’s wisdom, he has nothing he can actually apply to his own life.

Step 4: Make Choice / Take Action

“I’ll ask him directly.”

Prince visits Munir at the mosque and asks him how he consistently knows the right thing to do.

Step 5: Get Goal

“That actually makes sense…”

Munir explains that the guidance he follows comes from Islam. He tells Prince that the religion provides principles and advice for living a good life.

Prince recognizes that much of what Munir describes seems to be wise and beneficial. 

Step 6: Pay for Getting Goal / Major Conflict

“But how do you know Islam is actually true?”

Prince immediately asks this question.

Munir explains that Islam claims to come from the Creator—the One who created humanity and therefore knows what is best for it.

When Prince asks how anyone could know such a Creator exists, Munir does not ask him for blind faith. Instead, he invites Prince to investigate the claim for himself by reflecting on creation.

“Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators [of themselves]?”

“Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain.”

“And on the earth are signs for the certain [in faith], and in yourselves. Then will you not see?”

“Say, ‘Observe what is in the heavens and the earth.’”

Step 7: Make Pivotal Choice

“I’ll think about it.”

Instead of dismissing Munir’s argument, Prince decides to investigate it honestly. If the existence of a Creator really is the foundation for everything Munir believes, then that question deserves to be answered first.

Step 8: Changed Story World

“I will look into it.”

Prince commits to sincerely investigating the question of a Creator before deciding whether Munir’s guidance—and Islam itself—is true.
