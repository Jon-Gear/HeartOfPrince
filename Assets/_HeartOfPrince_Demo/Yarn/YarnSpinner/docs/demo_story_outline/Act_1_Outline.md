Step 1: Character

“Everything happened so fast…”

Prince is overwhelmed. Within the span of a few hours, he has suffered a heart attack, learned he has only two years left to live, and watched the future he imagined disappear. He has no idea how to process any of it.

Step 2: Goal

“I just need to stop thinking about it.”

Unable to cope with everything that has happened, Prince tries to occupy his mind with anything else. He isn’t looking for answers—he’s simply trying to escape his own thoughts.

Step 3: Problem

“There’s nothing to distract me.”

Confined to a hospital bed, Prince has nothing to do except think. The elderly man sharing his room cannot speak, leaving Prince alone with his racing thoughts.

Step 4: Make Choice / Take Action

“I might as well talk to him.”

Prince notices that the elderly man’s son visits every day and spends hours by his father’s bedside. Since he is the only person in the room Prince can really talk to, Prince strikes up a conversation with him.

Step 5: Get Goal

“At least it’s something else to think about.”

Prince succeeds in taking his mind off his own problems. Over several conversations, he learns that the man’s name is Munir. He gets to know his family, his background, his character, and the calm, respectful way he carries himself.

Step 6: Pay for Getting Goal / Major Conflict

“How does someone become like that?”

The more Prince observes Munir, the more he notices something he has never seen before: a man who seems to live with genuine purpose. Munir is kind, content, devoted to his family, and quietly wise. Rather than simply distracting Prince, Munir awakens a new question: what is the foundation of a life like his?

Step 7: Make Pivotal Choice

“I want to understand him.”

Prince decides that after leaving the hospital, he will seek Munir out. He wants to learn what shaped him into the man he is and whether there is something he himself has been missing.

Step 8: Changed Story World

“Maybe there’s another way to live.”

Prince leaves the hospital with a sense of direction. His diagnosis hasn’t changed, and neither have his problems, but he is no longer completely overwhelmed. For the first time since his heart attack, he knows the next step he wants to take: find Munir and learn from him.
