## 1. Topic statement

**Ponder about What Munir Has** (title: `WhatMunirHas`)

Prince reflects alone, at night in the hospital, on everything he has watched Munir do over the past days. He lists what he likes about him — caring for his parents, his manners, his strength and discipline, his family, his leadership, his contentment, his word. He realizes Munir is the first man he has ever met who looks like he has things figured out — and that none of it is luck. Munir's life is built on evidence, not mood. Prince wants to be like him. He has never met anyone like him before.

- Character: none (ponder)
- Direction: none
- Title candidates: `WhatMunirHas` (recommended), `MunirsBlueprints`, `AManWhoHasItFiguredOut`

## 2. Scene

After several days of hospital talks (Act 1 Step 5). Munir has just left for the night. Prince is alone in his bed, unable to settle — the same restless energy that used to make him walk the hideout. Entry line (Step 6): **"How does someone become like that?"**

The topic carries the arc of Steps 6–8:
- **Step 6 (conflict):** the observations build until Prince realizes he wants the *foundation*, not just the distraction
- **Step 7 (pivotal choice):** he decides to seek Munir out after leaving the hospital
- **Step 8 (changed world):** first direction since the heart attack — "Maybe there's another way to live."

## 4. What Prince notices about Munir (ranked — write in this order)

**1. He cares for his parents.** The daily visits, hours by a father who can't answer, gentle at the bedside with no one watching. Nobody in Prince's world serves someone who can't pay it back. *Grounding:* Qur'an 17:23–24, 31:14; "Your mother, your mother, your mother, then your father" (Bukhari/Muslim); "The pleasure of the Lord is in the pleasure of the parent" (Tirmidhi).

**2. Excellent manners — pleasant to be around.** Same courtesy for nurses, elders, kids, strangers, and Prince himself, from the first word. *Grounding:* Qur'an 68:4; "I was sent to perfect noble character" (Ahmad); "Nothing is heavier on the scales than good character" (Tirmidhi). Gentleness folds in here too: "Whoever is deprived of gentleness is deprived of all good" (Muslim).

**3. Strong and disciplined.** Weightlifting, hiking, straight posture; the discipline shows in the body and in the routine. A giant who never uses it. *Grounding:* "Your body has a right over you" (Bukhari); "The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer" (Muslim); "The strong one is the one who controls himself at the moment of anger" (Bukhari/Muslim).

**4. A family — an excellent father.** Patient and warm with five kids, never raising his voice; "patience is a language I learned at home." Prince's soft spot for children and his dream of being a father meet this head-on. *Grounding:* "He is not of us who does not show mercy to our young" (Tirmidhi); "The best of you are the best to their families" (Tirmidhi); the Prophet kissing children (Bukhari).

**5. A strong leader of his community.** Runs the masjid, keeps it maintained, the steady fixed point everyone leans on; a leader who serves rather than commands. The closest thing Prince has ever seen to his own role with the Alley Cats — but held together by love and service, not fear. *Grounding:* Qur'an 9:18; "Whoever builds a mosque for Allah, Allah builds a house for him in Paradise" (Bukhari/Muslim); "Allah is in the aid of His servant as long as the servant is in the aid of his brother" (Muslim). The garden can be a texture beat here (the seedling hadith: "If the Day of Judgment comes and one of you has a seedling in his hand, let him plant it" — Muslim).

**6. Content.** Doesn't chase, doesn't complain, carries a paralyzed father since thirteen without bitterness; at rest in the silence that Prince runs from. *Grounding:* "Richness is not in many possessions but in the contentment of the soul" (Bukhari/Muslim); "Amazing is the affair of the believer... if hardship befalls him he is patient, and that is good for him" (Muslim); Qur'an 2:153.

**7. A man of his word.** Same hour every day, a promise he never breaks; what he says happens, happens. Reliability Prince's world doesn't have. *Grounding:* "Fulfill the covenant; indeed the covenant is ever enquired about" (Qur'an 17:34); "The honest, trustworthy one is with the Prophets, the truthful, and the martyrs" (Tirmidhi).

**Folded-in traits:** gentleness lives inside #2 and #3; humility lives inside #5 (a leader who serves) and #6 (content with what he has).
